Add-On Service

Finale Showcase Day

Turn training into proof. Your team presents the workflows they actually built.

  1. Opening

    Trainer sets context: cohort profile, workflows submitted, tools most used. Voting criteria explained.

  2. Peer Showcase Circles

    Every participant presents their workflow in small groups of 6–8 (3 minutes each): the trigger, the before/after, and what they learned.

  3. Gallery Wall & peer vote

    All workflows displayed on screen. Participants and leadership vote using the four criteria below.

  4. Top 5 live demos

    The top-voted participants demo their working workflow live to leadership (8–10 min each incl. Q&A). Screen share of the real thing, not slides about it.

  5. Leadership response & next steps

    Recognition, strategic commentary, and how the team keeps iterating.

Voting criteria

FrequentTriggers daily or weekly.
MeasurableClear before/after.
Team-wideColleagues could adopt it.
Working, not conceptualIt actually runs.
