Use Case 1

Streamline task management

Create a checklist for opening and closing duties
I am a retail manager creating a structured daily operations checklist for opening and closing procedures. Using @[Onboarding New Hire Information], design a comprehensive template with columns for: Task Description, Opening Duties (with checkboxes), Closing Duties (with checkboxes), Priority Level (High/Medium/Low), Estimated Time, Responsible Person, and Completion Status. Include tasks covering: cash handling and register setup/closeout, security procedures and alarm systems, inventory checks and merchandise displays, staff scheduling and communication, cleaning and maintenance items, and safety protocols. Format as a printable checklist that ensures consistent daily operations and accountability
Write an email to communicate remaining tasks
I am a retail manager communicating shift handoff information to my team. Using @[Opening and Closing Duties Tracker], write a clear team email (100-150 words) that updates the incoming shift on outstanding tasks from the AM shift including: specific incomplete duties that need attention, priority levels for remaining tasks, any issues or challenges encountered during opening, time-sensitive items requiring immediate action, notes about customer or operational matters to be aware of, and clear assignments for who should handle each remaining task. Use a professional, solution-focused tone that ensures smooth shift transition and accountability

Use Case 3

Manage audits

Create a formula to calculate the difference between two columns
I am a [frontline manager/inventory coordinator/operations supervisor] analyzing inventory discrepancies between physical counts and system records. Create an Excel/Google Sheets formula to calculate the difference between [total number counted] and [quantity on hand] columns, identify items with discrepancies (positive or negative variances), and highlight significant differences that require investigation. Include: basic subtraction formula for variance calculation, conditional formatting to highlight discrepancies, filter or sorting suggestions to isolate problem items, and additional formulas to calculate percentage variance or flag items outside acceptable tolerance ranges
Write a message to a supervisor about an audit
I am a warehouse worker conducting an inventory audit and need to update my supervisor on audit findings. Write a professional message (75-100 words) that informs my supervisor that I’ve identified products with incorrect counts during the audit, provides a brief overview of the scope or number of discrepancies found, explains the next steps I’m taking to investigate and resolve the count issues, indicates timeline for completion of the investigation, and requests guidance or approval if needed. Use a clear, accountable tone that demonstrates proactive problem-solving and keeps management informed of audit progress.
Use Case 4

Improve team collaboration by finding and sharing information easily

Find a document about a specific policy
I am a [frontline manager/team supervisor/customer service lead] looking for our updated return and exchange policy documentation. Search for and locate the document containing our new return and exchange policies including: policy effective dates and key changes from previous policies, return timeframes and conditions, exchange procedures and limitations, required documentation or receipts, exceptions or special circumstances, refund processing timelines, and any employee guidelines for implementing these policies. If multiple versions exist, identify the most current official policy for team training and customer communication.
Summarize a policy document in an email
I am a [frontline manager/team lead/supervisor] writing to new team members about our updated policies. Using @[Updated Return and Exchange Policy H2 2024], draft a professional email (200-250 words) that welcomes new colleagues and summarizes key policy changes including: major updates from previous policy, return timeframes and qualifying conditions, exchange procedures and requirements, special circumstances or exceptions, implementation timeline and training resources, impact on daily customer interactions, and contact information for policy questions. Use a clear, informative tone that helps new team members understand and apply the policies effectively
Use Case 5

Find accurate information quickly

Find a document with specific details
I am a [frontline manager/team lead/supervisor] at [company name] looking for our [specific holiday – Black Friday/Christmas/Memorial Day] sale documentation. Search for and locate the document containing sale details including: promotion dates and duration, discount percentages or pricing, eligible products or categories, terms and conditions, employee guidelines or talking points, and any customer communication materials. If multiple versions exist, identify the most current and official version for team reference
Find out how much customers can save during a sale
I am a [customer service representative/sales associate/team member] helping customers understand savings opportunities during our current sale. Based on the available sale documentation or pricing information, calculate and explain how much customers can save on [specific product type/category] including: percentage discounts available, dollar amount savings ranges, any tiered pricing or volume discounts, additional promotions or bundle offers, and comparison to regular pricing. Present the information clearly for customer communication and include any relevant terms or restrictions.
