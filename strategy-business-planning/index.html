Use Case 1

Write a business plan

Write a comprehensive business plan
I am a [business owner/entrepreneur/strategic planner] developing a comprehensive business plan for [company name] in the [specific industry] targeting [detailed target market]. Create a professional business plan including: executive summary with company overview and value proposition, market analysis and competitive landscape assessment, detailed description of products/services: [list of products/services], marketing and sales strategy with customer acquisition plans, operational plan and organizational structure, management team and key personnel, financial projections: [specific financial projections] with 3-5 year forecasts, funding requirements and use of capital, risk analysis and mitigation strategies, and implementation timeline with key milestones. Format as a comprehensive document suitable for investors, lenders, or strategic planning purposes
Use Case 2

Generate a market research report

Generate a detailed market research report
I am a [business analyst/marketing manager/strategic planner] conducting comprehensive market research for the [specific industry] targeting [detailed target audience demographics/psychographics]. Create a detailed market research report analyzing competitors: [list of competitors] and including: industry overview with size, growth trends, and key drivers, target audience analysis with buying behaviors and preferences, competitive landscape with positioning, pricing, and market share, market opportunities and gaps, regulatory or technological factors impacting the industry, customer pain points and unmet needs, and strategic recommendations for market entry or expansion. Present with executive summary, data visualizations, and actionable insights.
Use Case 3

Generate a financial projection

Generate a 5-year financial projection for a new coffee shop
I am a [entrepreneur/business owner/financial planner] developing financial projections for a new coffee shop venture. Create a comprehensive 5-year financial model including: startup costs breakdown: [specific startup costs], projected revenue by stream: [revenue streams] with growth assumptions, annual operating expenses: [operating expenses] with inflation factors, monthly cash flow projections for Year 1, annual P&L statements for Years 1-5, break-even analysis and timeline, key financial ratios and performance metrics, and sensitivity analysis for different scenarios. Present in spreadsheet format with clear assumptions documented for each projection.
