Use active voice, specific details, and maintain professional yet engaging tone. Format with headings: ‘Press Release,’ ‘Alternative Headlines,’ ‘Distribution Strategy,’ ‘Target Media List,’ and ‘Social Media Adaptations
Use Case 2

Plan and track budgets

Identify trends and patterns in expenses
I am a [startup founder/CFO/financial analyst] analyzing our company’s expense patterns for strategic planning. Using the attached expense spreadsheet, conduct a comprehensive analysis of spending trends over the last three years including: expense category breakdown with year-over-year growth rates, identification of categories with significant cost increases (>15-20% annually), seasonal or cyclical patterns in spending, correlation between expense growth and business milestones or revenue, potential cost drivers (headcount growth, market expansion, technology investments), benchmarking against typical startup expense ratios, and actionable recommendations for cost optimization or budget reallocation. Present findings with data visualizations and executive summary suitable for board presentation.
Use Case 3

Develop your personal brand

Help me grow my personal brand
I am the founder of [startup name] in [specific industry] building my personal brand to generate media coverage and business growth. I’m passionate about [specific topics] and want to inspire [target audience] with entrepreneurial insights and startup lessons. Develop a personal branding strategy including: content pillars and messaging themes that showcase expertise, optimal social media platforms and posting strategies for my audience, thought leadership opportunities (speaking, writing, podcasting), networking and partnership approaches, content formats that resonate (stories, tips, case studies), and measurable goals with timeline for building following and media attention. Focus on authentic, value-driven approaches that establish credibility in my industry.
Use Case 4

Develop product strategy and roadmap

Clean a user feedback survey spreadsheet
I need to clean my user feedback survey spreadsheet with these specific transformations: replace all blank values in the ‘Name’ column with ‘Anonymous’, change ‘Yes’ to ‘Y’ in the ‘Recommend’ column, and remove any rows where the ‘Satisfaction’ column is blank. Process the attached spreadsheet file and generate a new cleaned version with these changes applied. Maintain all other data and column formatting, and provide a summary of how many rows were modified or removed during the cleaning process.
Build a high-level roadmap
I am head of product at [startup name] in the [specific industry] developing a Q4 delivery roadmap for [product name]. Based on recurring user feedback including [specific feedback trends], create a high-level roadmap table for implementing [specific features] with columns for: Feature/Initiative, Priority Level (P0/P1/P2), Quarter/Month Timeline, Key Milestones, Dependencies, Resource Requirements, and Success Metrics. Structure the roadmap to ensure Q4 delivery while addressing the most critical user feedback first. Include buffer time for testing and iteration, and highlight any potential risks or blockers.
Craft a synopsis of a product launch
Please craft a concise and impactful synopsis of the product launch using information from @[Product Launch – Notes], structured around three key points: (1) the product’s core value proposition or innovative feature set and how it solves a meaningful problem; (2) the target audience or market segment it is intended to serve, including why this group benefits most from the launch; and (3) the broader strategic significance of the product for the company, such as competitive differentiation, market expansion, or alignment with long-term business goals; ensure the tone is clear, compelling, and adaptable for internal stakeholders, media briefings, or promotional use, and that the structure allows for easy repurposing across multiple communication channels.
Create a slide describing a product
Please create a clear and visually engaging slide that describes what [product] is, using information from @[Product Launch – Notes], including: a concise product definition or positioning statement that is easily understood by a broad, non-technical audience; 3–4 bullet points highlighting the product’s key features or benefits; optional visual elements such as icons or diagrams to support comprehension; and a short tagline or headline that captures the essence of the product in a compelling way; ensure the tone is informative but accessible, the layout is clean and presentation-ready, and the content is appropriate for both internal stakeholders and external partners or media.
Use Case 5

Develop a product launch plan

Brainstorm different scenarios for a product launch plan
I am head of product at [startup name] in the [specific industry] preparing to launch [product name/description]. Analyze 4 launch scenarios combining pricing options [price point A vs. price point B] with timing options [December vs. January launch]. For each scenario, provide: market conditions and competitive landscape considerations, customer readiness and demand factors, pros and cons with potential risks and opportunities, resource requirements and operational impact, and expected outcomes for user acquisition and revenue. Additionally, suggest 3-4 alternative approaches we may not have considered, such as different pricing models, phased rollouts, or market entry strategies.
Compare product prices to competitors
I am a [product manager/pricing strategist/startup founder] analyzing competitive pricing for [our product category]. Research and compare pricing strategies of [specific competitor products] including: current pricing tiers and structures, promotional tactics (free trials, discounts, bundles), value-based positioning and messaging to [target audience], pricing psychology and anchoring strategies, and any recent pricing changes or market moves. Provide a structured comparison showing how our pricing [current/proposed prices] positions against competitors, identify pricing gaps or opportunities, and cite all sources used for the analysis. Focus on actionable insights for our pricing strategy.
Use Case 6

Create an elevator pitch (speech to text)

Create a short elevator pitch for a startup
I am the founder of [startup name] in the [specific industry] creating an elevator pitch for [specific audience – investors/customers/partners]. Develop a compelling 60-90 second pitch that includes: attention-grabbing hook that addresses a clear problem, solution overview highlighting [key product features], market opportunity and traction metrics, competitive advantage or unique value proposition, clear ask for [specific action], and anticipation of 2-3 likely follow-up questions with prepared responses. Use a professional yet conversational tone that demonstrates confidence while building credibility and urgency
Use Case 7

Conduct fundraising and investor relations

Draft a personalized email template to potential investors
I am a [founder/CEO/VP of Business Development] at [company name] reaching out to potential investors. Draft a personalized email template (200-250 words) that introduces our company and unique value proposition in [industry/market], highlights recent progress and key milestones on [specific initiatives], includes relevant traction metrics or achievements, demonstrates market opportunity and growth potential, and requests a meeting within the next month to discuss investment opportunities. Use a professional yet compelling tone with clear personalization placeholders for investor name, firm, and relevant background research.
Draft a thank you email to a potential investor
I am a [founder/CEO/business development executive] following up with a potential investor after our initial call. Draft a professional thank you email (100-150 words) that expresses appreciation for their time and interest, briefly recaps key discussion points from our conversation, acknowledges their specific [questions and concerns] and confirms we’ll address them comprehensively, proposes a follow-up meeting with suggested timeframes, and reinforces our enthusiasm about potential partnership. Use a confident yet respectful tone that maintains momentum from the initial conversation
Use Case 8

Communicate and negotiate with vendors

Create a comparison table for vendor proposals
I need to evaluate two packaging manufacturing vendor proposals from @[Company A’s Proposal] and @[Company B’s Proposal]. Create a comprehensive comparison table with key evaluation criteria: pricing (unit cost, setup fees, volume discounts), production capabilities (capacity, lead times, minimum orders), quality standards (certifications, materials, testing), service factors (customer support, delivery reliability, customization options), and contract terms (payment terms, warranties, flexibility). Include a scoring system (1-5 scale) for each criterion and calculate weighted totals to support decision-making. Format as a clear, printable comparison chart
Create an email draft to negotiate a bulk pricing discount
I am a [role – procurement manager/operations director] drafting an email to [selected vendor] confirming our decision to partner with them for [packaging services] while requesting a bulk pricing discount. Write a collaborative email (150-200 words) that expresses enthusiasm about the partnership, references specific strengths that led to our selection, presents our volume projections and bulk discount request with clear business rationale, and proposes next steps for finalizing terms. Use a professional yet friendly tone that positions this as a mutually beneficial discussion rather than a demand.
Use Case 9

Brainstorm business and strategy

Turn whiteboard notes into text
I am a founder at a [industry] startup and need to convert brainstorming whiteboard notes about [specific topic/challenge] into organized text. Transcribe all visible text from the attached whiteboard image and structure the content into: main themes or categories, key ideas under each theme, action items or next steps, and any questions or unresolved points. Present the information in a clear, scannable format with bullet points or numbered lists, and flag any text that’s unclear or illegible for confirmation
Suggest follow-up items for a brainstorm session
Based on our brainstorming session about [specific topic/challenge], identify 5-7 potential follow-up discussion items we may have overlooked. Analyze gaps in these areas: implementation challenges, resource requirements, timeline considerations, stakeholder impact, risk factors, and success metrics. For each suggested follow-up item, provide a brief rationale for why it’s important and indicate if it’s a critical gap or nice-to-have discussion. Focus on actionable next steps that could strengthen our original brainstorming outcomes.
