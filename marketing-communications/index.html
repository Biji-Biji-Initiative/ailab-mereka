Use Case 1

Perform audience research and develop personas

Conduct in-depth audience research
I am a marketing specialist at [company name] focused on [specific area/vertical] developing targeted campaigns for [specific personas]. Create a comprehensive audience research profile for [target audience] including: detailed demographics (age, income, location, education, job titles), psychographic insights (values, motivations, lifestyle, decision-making factors), digital behavior and preferred platforms (social media, content consumption, communication channels), key pain points and challenges that [product] addresses, messaging framework with language preferences and communication style, content formats and topics that engage them, and competitive analysis of how similar audiences are currently being targeted. Format as an actionable persona guide for campaign development.

Using @[Meeting Notes from Gemini], extract all action items and create a comprehensive project tracker with columns for: Task/Action Item, Assigned Owner, Priority Level (High/Medium/Low), Due Date, Status (Not Started/In Progress/Complete), Dependencies, and Notes/Context. Include any deadlines, deliverables, or follow-up items mentioned in the meeting. Format as a trackable spreadsheet that can be updated regularly and shared with the team for accountability and progress monitoring.

Use Case 4

Generate inbound marketing campaigns

Create compelling copy for a landing page
I am a [copywriter/marketing manager/digital marketer] creating a high-converting landing page for a [specific ebook/webinar/free trial] targeting C-suite executives and senior leaders. Write compelling copy including: attention-grabbing headline that addresses executive pain points, value proposition highlighting key benefits and outcomes, bullet points showcasing specific insights or solutions, social proof elements (testimonials, company logos, statistics), urgency or scarcity elements to drive immediate action, persuasive call-to-action buttons with action-oriented language, and brief form copy that minimizes friction. Use executive-appropriate language that demonstrates expertise while emphasizing ROI and strategic value.
Generate copy for a lead nurture email sequence
I am a [marketing automation specialist/email marketing manager/lead nurture strategist] creating a 5-email automated sequence for leads who downloaded our ebook on [specific topic]. Generate personalized email copy with: strategic timing intervals (immediate, day 3, day 7, day 14, day 21), progressive value delivery building on the ebook content, subject lines and preview text for each email, calls-to-action leading to [specific valuable resources/offers], personalization elements using lead data and behavior, content that addresses common questions or next steps, and conversion-focused messaging that moves leads through the sales funnel. Maintain consistent brand voice while increasing engagement and trust.

Write a brief, professional email (75-100 words) that presents [suggested email subject lines] for their review, provides context about the campaign or target audience, requests constructive feedback and suggestions by end of week, expresses appreciation for their expertise and collaboration, and includes clear next steps for incorporating their input. Use a collaborative, respectful tone that values their professional input
Use Case 6

Develop brand strategy

Define a brand architecture
I am a brand manager at [company name] developing a comprehensive brand architecture for our diverse portfolio. Create a structured brand framework that organizes our portfolio: [list all existing brands, products, and services] aligned with our mission and vision: [company mission and vision], target audiences: [specific target audience descriptions], and desired positioning: [market positioning statement]. Include: master brand hierarchy (corporate, sub-brands, product lines), brand relationship strategy (endorsed, freestanding, or hybrid), naming conventions and visual identity guidelines, brand portfolio rationalization recommendations, and implementation roadmap. Ensure the architecture supports both current offerings and future growth while maintaining brand clarity and market differentiation.
Use Case 7

Develop a visual identity

Generate ideas for a creative and eye-catching logo
I am a [business owner/entrepreneur] developing branding for a coffee shop and video game cafe hybrid. Generate logo concepts that balance both coffee and gaming elements without clutter, appealing to gamers (casual to enthusiast) and coffee enthusiasts seeking a unique social space. Create 3-4 concepts in each style: Modern and Playful (bold colors, pixel art elements, fun graphics), Retro-Cool (arcade-inspired chunky lettering, neon color palette), and Sleek and Minimalist (clean geometric shapes, subtle dual-theme references). For each concept, describe: visual elements and color scheme, how it represents both business aspects, target audience appeal, and scalability across applications (signage, merchandise, digital use).
Generate more logo options in a specific style
I am a [business owner/brand designer] developing additional logo concepts for my coffee shop and video game cafe. Create 3 more retro-cool style logo options that expand on the arcade-inspired aesthetic, featuring: chunky, bold lettering reminiscent of classic arcade games, neon-inspired color palettes (electric blues, hot pinks, bright greens), vintage gaming elements (joysticks, power buttons, retro controllers) integrated with coffee imagery (beans, cups, steam), and authentic 80s/90s arcade aesthetic. Each concept should clearly represent both the coffee and gaming aspects while maintaining the nostalgic, high-energy vibe that appeals to gamers and coffee lovers.
Use Case 8

Develop a competitive analysis

Conduct a competitive analysis for a new brick-and-mortar store

Conduct a competitive analysis of [key competitors] focusing on [specific focus area – customer experience/pricing/location strategy]. For each competitor, analyze: store format and layout strategies, pricing and promotional tactics, customer service approach and in-store experience, location selection and foot traffic patterns, strengths and competitive advantages, notable weaknesses or gaps, and measurable results or market performance. Provide actionable recommendations for [my company] to differentiate and succeed in the physical retail space, including specific tactics to leverage our online expertise.
Generate a competitive analysis of two companies
I am a [business analyst/marketing manager/strategic planner] conducting a competitive analysis comparing [company name] versus [competitor name] in the [specific industry/market segment]. Analyze both companies across key dimensions: market positioning and brand differentiation, product/service offerings and pricing strategies, target audience and customer segments, marketing and distribution channels, financial performance and market share, operational strengths and weaknesses, recent strategic moves or innovations, and competitive advantages or vulnerabilities. Present findings in a structured comparison format with actionable insights for strategic decision-making and market positioning

Create a detailed comparison table analyzing both proposals based on my requirements: twice-weekly service including vacuuming, mopping, dusting, window cleaning, and surface wiping. Use Case 13

Create and optimize copy for search engine marketing (SEM)

Generate a list of keywords for an SEM campaign
I am a digital marketing manager at [company name] creating an SEM campaign for [specific product/service]. Using my seed keywords: [list keywords], generate an expanded keyword list including: 15-20 additional relevant keywords, 10-15 long-tail keyword phrases (3+ words), competitor brand keywords, question-based keywords (how, what, where), buying intent keywords (buy, purchase, price), and negative keyword suggestions to avoid irrelevant traffic. Organize by match type recommendations (broad, phrase, exact) and include estimated search volume ranges and competition levels where possible
Refine ad copy for a specific audience
I am a [digital marketing manager/copywriter/advertising specialist] refining ad copy for a [specific campaign type] targeting [specific audience demographics/psychographics]. Create 6-8 new ad copy variations with a [specific tone] that prominently feature [specific product feature/benefit]. Each variation should include: compelling headlines (platform character limits), persuasive descriptions emphasizing the key feature, audience-appropriate calls-to-action, and messaging that resonates with [audience]’s pain points and motivations. Ensure copy speaks directly to this audience’s language and priorities while maintaining consistent brand voice.
Generate ad copy variations for an SEM campaign
I am a [digital marketing manager/SEM specialist/paid search coordinator] creating ad copy variations for a search engine marketing campaign promoting [specific product/service]. Using the provided keywords as inspiration, generate 6-8 ad copy variations with different approaches: headlines (25-30 characters), descriptions (80-90 characters), and calls-to-action. Use a [specific tone] throughout and ensure each variation tests different value propositions (price, quality, convenience, urgency). Format for Google Ads specifications and include keyword integration suggestions for each variation.

Use Case 19

Analyze social media trends and other data to reduce time to market

Research social media trends
I am a [role/title] at [company name/industry type] developing a [campaign type/strategy] for our [specific product category/service]. Research current social media trends across [platform names – Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, etc.] related to [specific topic/niche/hashtag]. Provide a comprehensive analysis including: trending hashtags and keywords with engagement metrics, top 5-10 influencers in this space with follower counts and engagement rates, content formats performing best (video, carousel, stories, etc.), common themes and messaging approaches in viral content, posting frequency and optimal timing data, and emerging sub-trends or niches within the topic. Present findings in a structured format with actionable insights for content strategy, include data sources and collection timeframe, and highlight opportunities for our brand to authentically participate in these conversations.
Analyse the findings in this report
Analyze the attached [report type – market research/customer survey/competitive analysis] focusing on insights relevant to [specific product/service] targeting [detailed target audience demographics/psychographics]. Identify and synthesize key themes, patterns, and data points related to [specific topic areas – customer pain points/market gaps/competitor strategies]. Provide a structured analysis including: top 3-5 recurring themes with supporting evidence from the report, quantitative insights and trends with specific metrics, qualitative patterns in customer feedback or market behavior, competitive positioning opportunities based on gaps identified, and actionable recommendations for messaging strategy. Present findings in priority order with clear rationale for how each insight directly impacts our [marketing campaign/product positioning/go-to-market strategy] for [target audience]. Include relevant quotes or data points from the report to support recommendations, and highlight any surprising or counterintuitive findings that challenge current assumptions.
